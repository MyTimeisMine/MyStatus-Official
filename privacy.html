Privacy Policy

Effective November 21, 2026

This policy explains how WenBin Han operates 還活著嗎？ and handles personal information in the iOS app.

Information we use

  • Sign-in and profile. We use Google Sign-In and Supabase Auth to verify your identity and maintain your session. Account information includes your email address and account identifier. Your profile stores the display name and profile ID you choose. Other signed-in users can find your profile using your exact profile ID.
  • Status and friends. We store your availability, status text, updates and expiry settings so confirmed friends can see your status. We also store friend requests, friendships, private nicknames, and notifications, including read status. Your nicknames for friends are visible only to you. Recent status shortcuts are stored on your device.
  • Time zone and country or region. If time zone sharing is enabled, confirmed friends can see your time zone. Country or region sharing is off by default. If you enable it and grant location permission, the app uses Apple location and geocoding services to determine your country or region. 還活著嗎？ sends the country or region code to its backend. Turning off sharing removes that code from your profile. You can manage location permission in iOS Settings.
  • Notifications. When push notifications are enabled, the app registers a device token so Apple Push Notification service can deliver friend notifications. You can manage push permissions in iOS Settings.
  • Feedback. If you submit feedback, we receive the text you enter and the diagnostics shown before submission, including account information, app and build version, iOS version, and device model. We use this information to investigate the issue.

Service providers and tracking

Google handles sign-in. Supabase provides authentication, database and backend services. Apple provides location and geocoding services and push notification delivery. These providers process information needed to deliver those functions. The app does not use advertising trackers or track your activity across other companies’ apps and websites.

Retention and account deletion

We keep account and service data as needed to operate the app and respond to feedback. Specific controls, such as turning off country or region sharing or removing a friend, remove the related data as described in the app. Uninstalling the app does not delete your account or its backend data.

To permanently delete your account, open your profile settings (設定個人資訊), select Delete Account (刪除帳號), and confirm. This removes your sign-in account, profile, status, friendships, nicknames, requests, received notifications, feedback, and registered push devices from 還活著嗎？. Existing notifications in other accounts lose the link to your deleted account. Local recent status and country or region settings associated with your account are cleared.

Privacy requests and contact

To ask about your information, request access, correction or deletion, or contact us about this policy, email support@statuship.app. Your rights depend on the laws that apply to you.
